這是什麼

NCCU GobiTrack 是政大 EMBA 戈壁挑戰隊（戈22）隊內使用的跑步訓練 App。隊員把 Garmin 帳號連接一次，跑步紀錄與每日健康數據就會自動進來；App 顯示配速、心率區間、週跑量與訓練負荷，教練在 App 裡看各隊員的狀況、開每週課表並推送到隊員的手錶。

About

NCCU GobiTrack is a private training app for the National Chengchi University EMBA team preparing for the Gobi Desert Challenge, a 4-day 120 km ultra-run. Members connect their Garmin account once; the app receives their running activities and daily health data and shows pace, heart-rate zones, weekly mileage and training load to the member and the team coaches. Coaches plan weekly workouts and send them to members' watches.

誰可以使用

只有在隊伍名冊上的隊員、教練與幹部可以登入。App 不對外開放，不收費，沒有廣告。
